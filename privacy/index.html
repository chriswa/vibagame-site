Privacy policy

vibagame collects nothing, and the developer receives no data from the app. It has no analytics, ads or accounts.

The app talks only to the vibagame app on your own Mac, which you pair it with. Your voice and game data go to that Mac.

Your Mac's AI agent uses your own Claude subscription, so what you say to it is handled by Anthropic under your agreement with them.

Questions: support@vibagame.com.
