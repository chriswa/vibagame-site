Privacy policy

vibagame collects nothing about you. Its Mac app, iPhone app and web page have no analytics, no ads and no vibagame accounts. We don't look at your data, and we have no interest in it.

Your voice, games and chats stay on your own Mac and the devices you pair with it. We never see them. The AI agent on your Mac sends what you say to it to the AI provider you use, which handles it under your agreement with them.

The only thing that reaches a server of ours is the Mac app's downloads and updates, which use a personal login and are logged so we can see who has downloaded what.

This policy covers vibagame running on your own Mac. Any later service that runs on our servers will have its own.
